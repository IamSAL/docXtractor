# **Automated Data Extraction Architecture for Derivative Warrants: A Comprehensive Schema Analysis**

## **The Paradigm of Derivative Warrants within the Stock Exchange of Thailand**

The proliferation of derivative warrants (DWs) across global emerging markets, particularly within the Stock Exchange of Thailand (SET), has fundamentally transformed retail and institutional equity participation. Derivative warrants operate as highly leveraged, non-collateralized financial instruments that grant the holder the right, though not the obligation, to acquire (via a Call DW) or divest (via a Put DW) an underlying asset at a mathematically predetermined exercise price, utilizing a specific exercise ratio, upon a predefined maturity date.1 The trading velocity of these instruments within the Thai financial ecosystem is staggering, with historical aggregate turnover frequently surpassing 1.7 trillion baht annually.1 This immense liquidity profile incentivizes major financial institutions, such as Bualuang Securities Public Company Limited, to continuously issue new tranches of derivative warrants to capture market demand, thereby generating an overwhelming volume of localized, unstructured financial documentation.2

The regulatory framework enforced by the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) of Thailand dictates strict issuance and disclosure parameters for these instruments. To qualify for listing on the SET, derivative warrants must possess a valid term not exceeding ten years from the issuance date, hold no transfer restrictions unless strictly mandated by legal articles, and feature an underlying asset that is free from severe trading halts or bankruptcy rehabilitation.4 To accelerate the time-to-market for these highly time-sensitive instruments, issuers leverage the SET's Direct Listing mechanism, which permits public offering via the exchange's trading mechanism without requiring prior syndication to market makers.2 Consequently, the term sheets, prospectuses, and pricing supplements generated during this accelerated listing process adhere to standardized, localized Thai-language templates.

For institutional data aggregators and financial engineering teams, the primary operational bottleneck lies in the ingestion of these unstructured or semi-structured Thai PDF term sheets and their subsequent transformation into a unified, globally standardized, machine-readable data matrix. The transition from manual data entry—which is prone to profound transcription errors and systemic formatting bugs—to an entirely automated, Artificial Intelligence (AI) driven extraction pipeline necessitates the development of a highly rigorous, semantically enriched JSON schema. This schema must not merely define computational data types; it must encapsulate the complex financial logic, the cross-lingual translation mapping, and the architectural defense mechanisms required to neutralize the structural anomalies historically embedded in legacy data formats.

## **Architectural Imperatives for Automated Data Ingestion**

The target architecture for the extracted data is a massive flat-file matrix containing precisely 238 distinct columnar fields.2 This extensive array indicates a "universal" global data model engineered to accommodate the absolute entirety of the structured financial products universe. A unified global schema of this magnitude is designed to catalog everything from standard European-style derivative warrants and heavily structured Credit-Linked Notes (CLNs) to complex Autocallable yield enhancement products and digital Dropback vehicles.2

Conversely, the source documents subjected to the extraction engine are localized Thai-language PDF term sheets detailing vanilla European-style derivative warrants.2 The underlying financial parameters of these specific Bualuang Securities instruments are structurally straightforward and highly deterministic:

- **Settlement Method:** The instruments mandate exclusive Cash Settlement (ชำระเงินตามส่วนต่างของราคาหลักทรัพย์อ้างอิง), meaning the physical delivery of the underlying shares is prohibited. The payout is strictly the monetary difference between the underlying asset's settlement price and the strike price, adjusted mathematically by the designated exercise ratio.2
- **Exercise Style:** The warrants feature a European exercise style (แบบยุโรป), indicating that automatic exercise occurs strictly and exclusively upon the maturity date, and only if the instrument concludes in-the-money.2
- **Collateralization:** The products are entirely non-collateralized and represent direct, unsecured obligations of the issuer. Consequently, their viability is intrinsically linked to the issuer's corporate credit rating, which for Bualuang Securities is denoted as AA by TRIS Rating Co., Ltd..2

Because the target data matrix is a unified global model comprising 238 fields, the vast majority of these columns will fundamentally remain null, false, or entirely blank for these specific vanilla Thai derivative warrants.2 The AI extraction model must be explicitly instructed, via the JSON schema's deep descriptive tags, to differentiate between core populated fields that require extraction and the massive array of extraneous fields that require strict structural nullification. Failure to strictly nullify these fields will induce LLM hallucination, where the AI might attempt to infer complex coupon schedules or autocall barriers from unrelated legal boilerplate within the PDF.

## **Forensic Analysis of Legacy Data Artifacts and Systemic Anomalies**

A critical prerequisite for formulating the JSON schema is conducting a forensic analysis of the legacy manual data entry file provided (test feb 26.xlsx \- test feb 26.csv).2 This historical dataset contains profound systemic data formatting artifacts and idiosyncratic logic chains that the AI must be carefully programmed to either replicate (if structurally required by downstream databases) or aggressively neutralize (if they represent historical human or software errors).

### **The Epoch Date Software Artifact**

The most severe data contamination within the legacy dataset involves the continuous, erroneous injection of the 1899-12-31 00:00:00 timestamp.2 An analysis of the first row of data (the BAM01C2608A Call Warrant) reveals that numerous high-index columns, which should structurally remain null for a vanilla warrant—such as leveraged_product_target_leverage_factor, put_strike_reference_asset_type, and call_strike_reference_asset_type—are instead populated with this specific timestamp.2

This is a globally recognized legacy artifact originating from spreadsheet software epoch configurations. In systems like Microsoft Excel, day zero is improperly calculated as December 31, 1899 (or January 1, 1900, depending on the specific Lotus 1-2-3 compatibility mode). When a manual data entry clerk leaves a numerical or referential cell blank, and a subsequent data pipeline forces a date-time formatting script over the entire array, these blank cells default to the integer zero, which the software then translates into 1899-12-31 00:00:00.2

Crucially, an analysis of subsequent rows in the identical dataset (such as Row 2 for CPALL01C2608A and Row 6 for CPF01P2608A) demonstrates that this artifact is not universal; in these rows, the exact same fields correctly remain null or entirely empty (represented by consecutive commas in the CSV syntax).2 The JSON schema must incorporate an absolute, unyielding directive instructing the AI extraction engine: _Under no circumstances should missing, blank, or structurally inapplicable data be substituted with epoch timestamps or default integers. Omitted fields must be returned as strictly null._

### **The Mathematical Overloading of Strike Level Fields**

The quantitative mechanics defining the issuance size and strike parity reveal a critical idiosyncrasy: the overloading of specific fields with unexecuted mathematical formulas rather than absolute numerical values.2

Standard financial data taxonomy dictates that columns named call_strike_level and put_strike_level should house the absolute exercise price of the option (e.g., 272.00 Baht for the ADVANC Put Warrant).2 However, the legacy database utilizes these specific columns to record the mathematical equation used to derive the principal_amount.2

For example, in Row 1 (BAM01C2608A), the call_strike_level field contains the exact string 20000000\*0.625. When this formula is executed, it equals 12,500,000, which perfectly matches the absolute value manually entered into the adjacent principal_amount column.2 This pattern repeats perfectly across the dataset: Row 2 (CPALL01C2608A) records 40000000\*0.08547 to yield a principal of 3,418,800; Row 4 (GLOBAL01C2608A) records 120000000\*0.25 to yield 30,000,000.2

Because downstream quantitative pricing engines or internal audit systems rely on this specific column containing the raw, unexecuted mathematical expression representing \[Quantity\] \* \[Issue Price\], the AI extraction model cannot simply execute the math and return the product, nor can it return the actual strike price in this specific field. The schema must explicitly instruct the AI to act as a precise string builder for these variables, concatenating the extracted 'จำนวนที่เสนอขาย' (Quantity) and the 'ราคาเสนอขายต่อหน่วย' (Issue Price) with an asterisk.

To ensure the Large Language Model successfully and consistently utilizes the aforementioned massive JSON schema without failure, the system prompt managing the data extraction architecture must incorporate several advanced behavioral directives.
